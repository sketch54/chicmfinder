CHICARMEET.US

A community calendar for car meets across the Chicagoland area.

Events are sourced from a public Google Calendar that is maintained by community members. If you know of a meet that's not listed, reach out to get it added.

HOW TO USE
- Browse today's meets in the sidebar, or pick any date with the calendar picker.
- Click any event to see its full details.
- Tap a pin on the map to highlight the corresponding event.
- Use the proximity sort button (bottom-right) to drop a pin anywhere on the map and instantly re-sort the list by distance to that point.
- Click the gear icon to swap in a different iCal calendar URL.

SUBMITTING A MEET
Contact us via social media or email to have your event added to the calendar. All meets welcome — cars, trucks, bikes, exotics, whatever.

DISCLAIMER
Event details are sourced from public calendar data and may change. Always verify time and location with the event organizer before heading out.
